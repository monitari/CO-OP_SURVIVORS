## Echo

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single game cat portrait icon. Image 1 is style reference only: match its chibi front-facing head proportions, thick dark navy outline, small solid navy oval eyes without irises, pink nose, tiny W smile, softly shaded flat 2D illustration. Generate ONE new cat Echo, an Abyssinian with warm ruddy brown ticked coat (no orange tabby stripes or calico patches), large ears, cream muzzle, teal collar with small fish-shaped returning-toy charm. Centered entire head and collar, 10 percent padding on all sides, square canvas, genuinely transparent background with alpha. No text, frame, glow, other cats, scenery, realistic fur or 3D. Must visually belong beside the existing ten portraits.
